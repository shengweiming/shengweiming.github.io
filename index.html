Abstract

This is my website. It contains my academic information—research, publications, and writing—as well as some other information about me. I am a PhD student working on the philosophy of artificial intelligence, with particular interest in how we should interpret the apparent mental lives of language models. The remainder of this document is organized as follows: Section 1 introduces the author, Section 2 surveys the author’s publications, Section 3 collects informal writing, and Section 4 describes how to get in touch.
